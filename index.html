The checklist I edit with

  1. Can a reader repeat the main point in one sentence?
  2. Does every number have a source next to it?
  3. Would you say each sentence out loud to a client?
  4. Is there a specific example, not just a claim?
  5. Did you cut the opening line and the recap at the end?
